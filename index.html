ABOUT ME

I'm a Unity game developer based in Copenhagen, currently pursuing a Master's in Games at the IT University of Copenhagen. I've spent the last five years building, shipping, and leading game projects, from co-founding a startup to managing junior devs as a lead programmer.

My journey started with a Bachelor's in Computer Science and a startup incubator that turned into Empty Box, a company where I took a game from concept to release on Steam. From there I moved on to Runic Dices Entertainment as Lead Unity Developer, overseeing architecture, scheduling, and mentoring a team of 3–5 developers.

I thrive in fast-paced environments where creative problem-solving meets technical execution. Whether it's designing movement systems, building roguelite mechanics, or presenting at industry events, I'm always looking for the next challenge.
